# Acerola — Proposed Sponsorship Milestones

This is a proposed validation plan, not a claim that funding has been secured or that dates are committed. Agree dates and budget with the sponsor after estimating actual costs.

| Phase | Work | Deliverables | Acceptance evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Baseline | Inventory features, environments, provider costs and known defects | Product status report and cost baseline | Dated report, reproducible demo |
| 2. Reliability | Test chat, auth, memory, attachments, provider fallback and media functions | Regression suite and defect register | Test logs and resolved critical defects |
| 3. Security | Review authorization, data isolation, secrets, rate limits and retention | Data-flow map and security remediation plan | Review findings and closure evidence |
| 4. User validation | Recruit a small, consent-based pilot group | Feedback report and usage dashboard | Defined metrics, anonymized results |
| 5. Commercial validation | Verify checkout, subscriptions, support and unit costs | Payment lifecycle report and operating budget | Reconciled transactions and cost evidence |
| 6. Sponsor review | Compare outcomes against agreed targets | Final milestone and spending report | Sponsor sign-off and next-stage decision |

## Metrics to establish
Do not publish a metric until its definition, source and reporting period are documented.

- Weekly active users: unique consenting accounts with at least one qualifying product action in a calendar week.
- Returning-user rate: share of a defined cohort that returns in a later period.
- Successful task completion: share of tested tasks completed without a blocking error.
- Chat success rate: successful completed requests divided by eligible requests, excluding documented user cancellations.
- Median response time: measured from request submission to completed response, with the measurement method stated.
- Cost per successful request: attributable AI and infrastructure cost divided by successful requests.
- Paid conversion and recurring revenue: based only on reconciled, genuine transactions, excluding test payments and refunds as appropriate.
